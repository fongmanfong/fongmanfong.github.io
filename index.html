ORIGIN

Before the pipelines, before the dashboards, there was a question.
"What is real? And how would you even know?"
Most people hear that question and move on. This one never did. The universe felt like a puzzle constantly emitting signals, and truth felt like the reward waiting for anyone patient enough to read them carefully.

THE PATH

Philosophy sharpened the mind. But philosophy without data is just poetry. Data without philosophy is just noise.
Data became the bridge. Not spreadsheets — a belief system. The conviction that humanity's most honest mirror is the trail it leaves behind in numbers. And data engineering? That's not a job title. That's building the mirror itself, so you can actually look.

THE ARENA

Ten years. Two of Canada's most consequential tech companies, Shopify and Wealthsimple. Where millions of merchants and investors put real stakes on the line, every day. This character didn't just work there. They helped those companies see themselves clearly, and grow because of it. Clarity, at scale, is a superpower most companies never find.

ABILITIES
  • Probabilistic Sight — Reads uncertainty as information, not noise. Bets on distributions, not outcomes.
  • System Architect — Sees how the parts connect before the first brick is laid. Builds for scale from day one.
  • Truth Seeker — Allergic to comfortable fictions. Follows the signal wherever it leads.
  • Growth Catalyst — Makes companies legible to themselves. That clarity becomes compound growth.
